## What to do next

Ranked by what a player would feel, not by what is cheapest or most interesting. Every row is one of
the mod's own stated goals failing to reach the player.

**Seven rows closed on 2026-08-26 — `C43`, `B43`, `B44`, `F28` and `C33`'s silent half — and what
is left at the top is the same walk it was.** The survey is **resuming** rather than restarting:
`C43` moved `Loops.xml` under a walk a third done, and twelve re-walked ships reproduce its rows to
the digit on all 22 columns, so the dataset keeps what it has (`E7`).

**`A13` closed on 2026-08-29 and every dataset on disk is re-taken**: the census, the stiffness
walk, the survey, the air walk, the paired cap walk and the paired floor walk. The blueprint reader
had built eleven kinds of block as plain armour, so every figure this page quoted about the
population understated what a ship makes. What the re-take found, four times over on four
independent mechanisms, is one shape: **block identity is worth 15.76 % of the population's heat and
about a per cent of its cost.** So every selection made on cost barely moved — the floor walk's own
selection went 294 ships to 283 with 282 shared — and every price paid in kelvin roughly doubled:
the cap's Δpeak p99 0.2820 K to 0.5660 K, the floor's 27.67 K to 60.20 K.

**Both approximations still fail and both decisions are unchanged.** `G6`'s cost half fails at
**1.80×** the allowance on 712 of 32,548 runs. A world cap of 6 is refused at a p99 Δpeak of
0.5660 K against a 0.6 K threshold, and the floor aimed only where the budget binds is refused at
60.20 K. Both ship as switches for a world that wants them, and what the re-take changed is the
margin rather than the verdict: the cap now sits a hair under the figure that would refuse it
outright, and a third of the floor's engaged cells exceed that figure rather than a twelfth.

**The `K` milestones are one rebuild now.** On 2026-09-24 the owner set the aerodynamic goal as a
realistic flight model, `K25`, and its first step stripped the force the mod applied so it can be
rebuilt from a surface profile of its own; what is left of `K` beside it is a cheap client that needs a
session and a debug convenience.

So what is left below is mostly what it always was: the rows blocked on something a lab cannot
reach.
